WidiAI

Piano audio to MIDI with AI

Year
2026
Context
Team project
Focus
Music transcription

A web app that listens to a piano recording and writes it out as MIDI you can download and play back in the browser.

  • Transcribes with two models: transkun and a custom Onsets and Frames network in PyTorch.
  • FastAPI backend runs transcription in a background thread pool and deletes temporary files after each request.
  • Recordings stay in the browser’s IndexedDB and are only sent to the server to be transcribed.

Path so far

  1. 2026 – nowResearch

    Do source-separation models leave AI-like fakeprints?

    Bachelor’s thesis, Universitat Pompeu Fabra

    • AI-music detectors look for spectral peaks left by transposed-convolution decoders. Many source separators, like HT-Demucs, use the same decoders, which could make separated human music look AI-generated.
    • Predicting each separator’s peak frequencies from its upsampling configuration and Fourier theory, then verifying them on separated MUSDB and FMA music.
    • Comparing waveform-decoder separators (Demucs family) with mask-based ones (Open-Unmix style), and measuring how often fakeprint and CNN detectors flag remixed and karaoke-style human music as AI.
    • Testing a fix: a separator-aware detector with a human / generated / separator-processed class or hard negatives.
